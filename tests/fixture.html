📜 BATTLE MANUAL

Welcome to Pixel Dungeon. Your goal is to defeat endless waves of monsters. You lose if your HP reaches 0.

Core Loop: Pick a Class ➡ Fight ➡ Win ➡ Draft Items to upgrade stats ➡ Repeat.

🦸 HERO CLASSES

🛡️ Warrior (Tank)
Starts with Bonus Shield. Ult deals dmg based on your Armor.
🪓 Berserker (Risk)
High Dmg, but takes +25% Dmg. Ult hurts self to deal double dmg.
🗡️ Rogue (Combo)
Gains Energy fast. Ult triggers an Instant Extra Turn.
🏹 Archer (Sniper)
20% Dodge Chance. Ult Ignores Armor (Direct HP Dmg).
🧙 Mage (Scaler)
Ult gains +10 Permanent Power every time you cast it.

📊 STATS EXPLAINED

⚔️ Sword: Damage per tile.
🛡️ Shield: Armor gain per tile.
💖 Heart: Healing per tile.
⚡ Energy: Ult Charge % per tile.
💀 Skull Damage: The Damage you deal to the opponent using skulls
☠️ Skull Self Damage: The Damage you deal to yourself using skulls
💥 Ult Power: Base damage of Ultimate.
🧛 Life Steal: % of Dmg dealt returns as HP.

🔥 COMBOS & TURNS (CRITICAL!)

Mastering combos is the only way to survive high levels.

Match 3 1x Effect. Turn passes to Enemy.
Match 4 2x Effect + EXTRA TURN.
L / T Shape 2.5x Effect + EXTRA TURN.
Match 5 3x Effect + ULT +%30 + EXTRA TURN.
Match 6 3x Effect + ULT +%60 + EXTRA TURN.
Match 7 3x Effect + ULT +%90 + EXTRA TURN.

⚡ SPEED BONUS

On your turn, a countdown starts the moment you regain control. The faster you make your swap, the bigger the multiplier on that move's effect (and any chain reaction it causes) - up to x2.0.

No Pressure: Letting the window run out never costs your turn - the bonus just fades back to a neutral x1. Play fast for bigger hits, or take your time. This bonus is yours only; enemies don't get it.

🧠 ENEMY AI

  • Minions: Make random moves. 30% chance to miss a good move.
  • Bosses (Lvl 5, 10...): Highly intelligent. They prioritize Match-4s and Skulls.
  • Independent Stats: Enemies have their own Sword/Shield/Heal/Energy/Skull stats that scale up with level (and spike further on bosses) - your own loot upgrades only make you stronger, not them.
  • Armor: If an enemy has Armor (Grey bar), you must break it before you can hurt their HP (Red bar).
  • Energy Denial: Enemies CANNOT use Ultimates. If they match ⚡ Energy, they Heal instead. Don't leave Energy for them!

🎁 REWARDS & LOOT

After winning, you pick items. The quality depends on your performance:

  • Flawless (100% HP): Pick 3 Items (Best for scaling).
  • Decent Win (75%+ HP): Pick 2 Items.
  • Standard Win: Pick 1 Item.
  • Near Death (<10% HP): Pick 3 Items (Comeback mechanic).
  • Last Stand (<1% HP): Pick 5 Items (Comeback mechanic).
  • Boss Kill Bonus: +1 extra Item on top of the above.
Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary.
Rest: Before each new level you recover 50% of your missing HP.
